What to decide

1 · Which rendering? A is done and free to keep as a secondary view. B and C are the same kind of thing, a smartlog webview, in two dialects; they cost about the same and can share one render layer. The choice inside the smartlog is between a focused-row panel with buttons (B) and a glyph-and-pill language read at a glance (C); a hybrid, C's rows over B's panel, is a real option. Either pulls "all stacks" (M9) forward, because a smartlog with one stack is a straight line.
2 · Which indicators are worth their pixels? The table is the candidate list, not the plan. The ones you named, a PR badge and "local is ahead of the PR branch", arrive with M5 and M6 regardless of view. Pick the row set; everything else stays in tooltips.
3 · When? M4 ships v0.1 on the tree and is view-independent. M6's per-layer actions are where the view starts to shape the code, so the pick should land before M6: a short "view lab" stack after M4 that builds the chosen rendering (or both B and C as prototypes on one shared model) against real data.
